# FAQ and compatibility

**My walkie-talkie on the belt hears nothing in singleplayer.**
Check that it is switched on, tuned to a station that is broadcasting, with volume above zero and a charged battery, and that the option *Listening at the belt* is on. It must be hooked on the belt, not stored in a bag.

**Can I talk with a walkie-talkie on the belt?**
No: take it in hand or wear a radio on your back.

**My adapted radio does not recharge.**
It needs a battery inside, and either a generator powering the square or grid power inside a building. Outdoors, grid power alone is not enough.

**I picked up an adapted HAM radio and it lost its adaptation.**
This happened with version 1.1.0 only. Craft *Adapt Radio for Mains Power* on it again.

## Compatibility

- **TooltipLib:** required.
- **Military Drop, Operation Artemis, Radio Survivor:** require this mod; it is their shared walkie-talkie receiver.
- **Better Walkie Talkies:** detected. While it is enabled, it keeps control of the battery at the belt and this mod adds no multiplayer bubble; singleplayer listening at the belt still works.
- Other mods that change radios at the belt or the radio window may conflict; this has not been checked.
