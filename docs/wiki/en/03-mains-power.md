# Mains power

Adapt a radio so that, in an electrified area, its battery no longer drains and recharges.

## Recipe: Adapt Radio for Mains Power

| | |
|---|---|
| Skill | Electrical 1 (known from the start) |
| Tool (kept) | a screwdriver |
| Consumed | 2 Scrap Electronics, 1 Electrical Wire |
| Experience | 10 Electrical XP |
| Radios | Toys-R-Mine, ValuTech, Premium Tech., Tactical, US Army and Makeshift walkie-talkies; Premium Technologies, US ARMY COMM. and Makeshift HAM radios; US ARMY COMM. Manpack Radio |

The radio is kept: channel, battery and identity do not change. A radio already adapted cannot be adapted again.

![The recipe in the crafting window](../../images/screenshots/mains-recipe.png)

## How it works

- **Electrified area:** a generator powers the square, or grid power reaches it inside a building (a room).
- There, an adapted radio **does not drain** its battery and **recharges** it, switched on or off, up to full. An empty battery is full after **10 in-game hours** by default (option *Mains recharge time*).
- Elsewhere, the battery drains normally.
- **A battery must be inside.**
- Works for a radio **carried** (hand, back, belt: your square counts) and **placed** (dropped on the ground, or placed as furniture like HAM radios: the radio's square counts).
- A HAM radio placed as furniture and picked up again stays adapted.
- Time spent outside the game, or while a placed radio's area is not loaded, is not caught up.

## Tooltip

The item tooltip shows **Adapted for mains power**, and **Charging on mains power** while it recharges.
