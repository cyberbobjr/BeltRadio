# Sandbox options

Page **Belt Walkie-Talkie** of the sandbox options. They are read each time they are used: a change in the middle of a game applies at once. An on/off option set to off gives back the vanilla behaviour.

| Option | Default | When on | When off (vanilla) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bubble for an unheld radio (multiplayer) | on | a line received by a walkie-talkie on the belt also shows in a radio bubble | the line only shows in the radio chat |
| Battery drain at the belt | on | the battery drains every minute at the belt | the charge stays frozen at the belt; the drain is caught up when it is taken in hand again |
| Listening at the belt (singleplayer) | on | a walkie-talkie on the belt hears its station | only a walkie-talkie in hand or a radio on the back hears anything |
| Noise of a walkie-talkie at the belt | on | noise that attracts zombies at each line received | noise only for the first line |
| Sound of a walkie-talkie at the belt | on | radio sound as in hand (static, broadcast sound) | no sound at the belt |
| Mains recharge time (hours) | 10 | in-game hours to recharge an empty battery on mains power, 1 to 240 | — |

Device Options, the window that stays open and the mains recipe do not depend on any option.
