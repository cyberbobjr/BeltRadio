# Belt Walkie-Talkie

[Français](../fr/Home.md)

Belt Walkie-Talkie is a Project Zomboid Build 42.21 mod (singleplayer and multiplayer). Hook your walkie-talkie on your belt and keep listening: it can be tuned, stays on while its window is open, hears its station in singleplayer, shows a radio bubble in multiplayer, drains its battery, plays the radio sound and makes noise, as in hand. Walkie-talkies and HAM radios can also be adapted for mains power.
