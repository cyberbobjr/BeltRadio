description=[h1]Belt Walkie-Talkie[/h1]
description=
description=[b]Build 42.21 · Singleplayer & Multiplayer · No other mod required[/b]
description=
description=Hook your walkie-talkie on your belt and keep listening. In vanilla, a walkie-talkie on the belt cannot be tuned, switches off as soon as its window is open and hears nothing in singleplayer. This mod fixes that, with your hands free.
description=
description=[h2]What it does[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Device Options[/b] on a walkie-talkie hooked on the belt: tune it, switch it on, set the volume.
description=[*]Its window no longer switches it off, even while you take it in hand.
description=[*][b]Singleplayer:[/b] a switched-on walkie-talkie on the belt hears the station it is tuned to (vanilla stations, emergency broadcast, stations added by mods), with the radio bubble above your character. Storms scramble it like a walkie-talkie in hand.
description=[*][b]Multiplayer:[/b] a line received by a walkie-talkie hooked on your belt also shows in a radio bubble above your character, not only in the radio chat.
description=[*]The battery drains at the belt, as in hand.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]What it does not do[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]You cannot transmit from the belt: take the walkie-talkie in hand (or wear a radio on your back) to talk.
description=[*]A radio stored in your inventory or in a bag hears nothing in singleplayer, as in vanilla.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Sandbox options[/h2]
description=
description=Page [b]Belt Walkie-Talkie[/b], all on by default; turned off, each one gives back the vanilla behaviour, even in the middle of a game:
description=[list]
description=[*]Bubble for a walkie-talkie on the belt (multiplayer).
description=[*]Battery drain at the belt.
description=[*]Listening at the belt (singleplayer).
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Shared receiver of [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3811752923]Military Drop[/url] and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3813648615]Operation Artemis[/url]: from their next update, they use it when it is enabled.
description=[*]Better Walkie Talkies is supported: it keeps its own battery and voice handling.
description=[*]Other mods can add their stations and colours through a small Lua API.
description=[/list]
